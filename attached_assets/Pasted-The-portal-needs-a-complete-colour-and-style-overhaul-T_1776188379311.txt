The portal needs a complete colour and 
style overhaul. The brown looks horrible. 
Replace everything with a clean, modern, 
luxury aesthetic that looks like a 
high-end SaaS product — think Notion 
meets a luxury beauty brand.

TARGET LOOK:
Sidebar: deep charcoal/near-black with 
soft white text and a single blush/rose 
gold accent colour.
Main area: very light warm grey background.
Cards: pure white, clean shadows, 
minimal borders.
Accent colour: a sophisticated dusty rose 
/ soft blush — not pink, not brown.
Typography: clean, modern, elegant.

COLOUR PALETTE — replace ALL brown:

PRIMARY ACCENT: #D4A5A5 (dusty rose/blush)
ACCENT DARK: #B8887E (muted terracotta for hover)
ACCENT GLOW: rgba(212, 165, 165, 0.15)

SIDEBAR BG: #1C1C1E (near-black charcoal)
SIDEBAR DARKER: #141416 (brand area top)
SIDEBAR BORDER: rgba(255,255,255,0.06)
SIDEBAR TEXT: #A8A8B3 (soft grey)
SIDEBAR TEXT ACTIVE: #FFFFFF
SIDEBAR ACTIVE BG: rgba(212,165,165,0.12)
SIDEBAR ACTIVE BORDER: #D4A5A5

MAIN BG: #F7F7F8 (cool light grey)
CARD BG: #FFFFFF
CARD BORDER: #EBEBED
CARD SHADOW: 0 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)

TEXT PRIMARY: #1C1C1E
TEXT SECONDARY: #6E6E80
TEXT MUTED: #A8A8B3
